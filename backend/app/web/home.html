What you get

One page that answers "Is my child okay?"

Everything that needs attention, in one place

  • Every course's average and whether it's going up or down
  • Alerts for missing work, low test scores and grade drops
  • "How to help": does it look like effort or understanding?
  • What's due in the next 7 days

A weekly update written for busy parents

Every week, a short summary of what changed: grade moves, new missing work, tests graded and tests coming up. It leads with what matters and says so plainly when it was a quiet week.

Right in WhatsApp

No app to install. Alerts and the weekly update arrive in WhatsApp, and you can ask questions there like you'd text a friend. The full overview is on the website whenever you want more detail.

The numbers come from rules, not from the AI.

Every grade, count and judgement is worked out by plain, checkable rules from your child's Schoology data. The AI only puts the results into words, so it can't invent a grade, and it never blames your child.

Start in about two minutes

Three steps, done once

  1. Register

    Enter your WhatsApp number and your child's Schoology address. Your child copies a key from their Schoology /api page, for example schoology.coppellisd.com/api.

  2. Send the JOIN code

    The page shows a short code. Send it to Parent Insight on WhatsApp. That confirms the number is yours.

  3. Ask anything

    Ask "What's due this week?" or "Why did Math drop?". Alerts and a weekly update arrive on their own.

Straight answers

What it can and can't do

It can

  • Show averages, changes, missing, late and upcoming work
  • Alert you when something needs attention
  • Write a weekly summary in plain English
  • Answer questions about schoolwork, naming its source
  • Spot skills your child keeps losing marks on
  • Read photos of marked tests and give advice for each

It can't

  • Change anything in Schoology. It only reads.
  • Answer questions outside schoolwork
  • See what teachers didn't put in Schoology
  • Show the school calendar or your child's timetable
  • Replace a conversation with the teacher
Questions parents ask

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. It's free while it's in testing and I gather feedback from families.

Is this made by the school or by Schoology?

No. It's an independent project by a student developer. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Schoology or any school district.

Why do I need an API key?

A key lets the app read your child's grades without ever seeing a password. You create it yourself on Schoology, and you can turn it off at any time on the same page.

Which students is it for?

Middle and high school students at US schools that use Schoology. While it's in testing, it's designed for students aged 13 and over.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything happens in WhatsApp, which you already have. The website is only for registering, and for the full overview if you want it.

How do I stop or delete everything?

Send STOP on WhatsApp to pause updates, or DELETE to remove your account and all its data. You can also delete it on the website at Delete my data.

Can the AI get things wrong?

The numbers come from rules, not the AI, and every answer names what it was based on, so you can check it on the overview. The AI's explanations are suggestions to talk through, not diagnoses.

Can I try it without connecting my child?

Yes. On the sign-in page, choose "Try the demo with a sample child" to explore with Maya, a made-up student.

Privacy

What happens to your data

Short version: your key is stored encrypted only to read your child's schoolwork, nothing is sold or shared for ads, and you can delete everything at any time.

What is stored

  • Your child's courses, assignments, grades, due dates and teacher comments, copied from Schoology so the app can work out averages and alerts
  • The Schoology key you register with, encrypted, so updates can be fetched while you're away
  • Your WhatsApp number, and your name and email from the Schoology account
  • The AI's summaries, explanations and your conversation, so it can follow up

What is not stored

  • Your Schoology password. The app never sees it.
  • Photos of marked papers. They're read and then discarded; only the marks you confirm are kept.

Who sees it

  • Only you. It is never sold, shared or used for ads.
  • To write summaries and answers, your child's school data is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI, used only to answer for you.
  • Messages travel through WhatsApp, run by Meta.
  • Data is kept in a secure database (Supabase, hosted in Ireland), and the app runs on Render.

Your controls

  • Send STOP on WhatsApp to pause updates, START to resume
  • Send DELETE on WhatsApp, or use Delete my data, to remove everything
  • Turn off the key any time on your child's Schoology /api page

Good to know

  • It's still in testing. Features may change as families give feedback, and you may find rough edges.
  • The demo uses made-up data and is deleted after a day.
